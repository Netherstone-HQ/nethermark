Works on touch screens. Tapping a toolbar button or menu keeps focus and the selection in the editor, so a phone's keyboard stays up. A pressed button shows its label, in place of the hover tooltip. Menus open toward the side with more room and scroll when taller than the space below. Hover colors only apply where there's hover. Escape in the editor closes a menu opened by a click or tap.
