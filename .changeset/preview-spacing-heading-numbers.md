The preview has room around its text, so it no longer sits flush against the editor, and its code, quotes and table cells get matching padding. Line numbers beside headings sit on the heading's baseline instead of at the top of the taller line. The current line's number uses the text color, so the other numbers read quieter. Gutter cells beside a heading carry `nme-gutter-h1` to `nme-gutter-h6`.
